Up and running in three steps

No code, no spreadsheets. Everything is configured from inside your CRM.

Install from the App Marketplace

Install LoyaltyLoop on your agency or a single sub-account. We create a Loyalty Points Balance field on your contacts for you.

Choose how customers earn and redeem

Pick from ready-made rules: points per dollar, review bonuses, appointment attendance, welcome bonuses and tag-based redemptions.

Let it run

Points are added the moment a customer pays or attends. Use the balance and VIP tags in your workflows, emails and smart lists.

Everything you need for a rewards programme

Built for agencies and local businesses that run on LeadConnector.

Flexible earn rules

Award points for paid invoices (any rate), appointment status, new contacts, or whenever a tag is applied.

Simple redemption

Add a tag like redeem_coffee and LoyaltyLoop deducts the points, removes the tag, and refuses if the balance is too low.

Automatic VIP tiers

Tag contacts the moment they cross a points threshold, then trigger premium follow-ups from your workflows.

Balance on every contact

The points total is written to a custom field, so it works with merge fields, filters and conditions everywhere in the CRM.

Workflow webhook

Award or deduct points from any automation with one webhook action, with built-in protection against duplicates.

Isolated per location

Each sub-account has its own rules, balances and history. Nothing is shared between locations.
